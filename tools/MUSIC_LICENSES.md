# Music licenses

Background beds used by `add_voiceover.py`. Tracks must be genuinely
royalty-free (CC0 or an equivalent license that allows commercial social
posts without attribution). Prefer originals so Instagram is not at risk
of a third-party Content ID claim.

## soft-tech-bed.mp3

- **Title:** Soft Tech Bed
- **File:** `tools/music/soft-tech-bed.mp3`
- **Source:** Original instrumental synthesized for the k4coding
  social-media-assets project. Generated by
  `tools/music/generate_soft_tech_bed.py` (Python + ffmpeg). No
  third-party samples, loops, or recorded performances were used.
- **License:** [CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)
  (public-domain dedication)
- **Duration:** 16 seconds (looped and trimmed to the Reel length)
- **Notes:** Quiet tech/corporate bed (soft kick, bass, pad, light
  arpeggio). Intended to sit under an AI voiceover. The mixer ducks it
  about 16 dB below the voice, then sidechain-ducked, with a short fade in and fade out.
  Regenerating the file from the script is deterministic (fixed seed).

Pixabay and other stock tracks were considered but skipped when they
were marked Content ID registered, which can mute or claim Instagram
Reels.
